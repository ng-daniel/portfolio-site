This was my first weekend-long game jam (previous ones were 1-2 weeks),
and I was really happy with how it came out! I ended up getting 5th overall,
2nd in use of limitation, and 3rd in enjoyment (which is most important
in my opinion!).

The theme/limitation was one button, multiple actions, so I made an asteroid defense
game where your character is always moving, but pressing space launches a fireball
and flips your movement direction.

Gameplay alternates between spamming spacebar to knock out a dense chunck of asteroids
and timing a few spacebar hits to knock out small asteroids that are far apart.

I also liked the feel of the fast-falling asteroids, which require the player's
immediate attention, causing them to book it across the entire map to destroy it
before it hits the planet.
