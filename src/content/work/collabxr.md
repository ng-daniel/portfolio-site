CollabXR is one of the projects I am working on as part of my part-time work
at the Purdue Envision Center.

I'm currently developing a major overhaul of the environment system supporting custom scene publishing and
dynamically loading scenes from remote repositories.

This should significantly improve the potential of environments as a more interactive feature
while reducing APK build sizes.

Some of the other work I've done include general maintenance and bugfixing,
documenting setup and dev workflows for users/contributors, and helping run live demos and workshops.
